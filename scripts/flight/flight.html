Low Flight Atlas · a small plane over four landscapes, cut as a colour woodblock print.

Render a video

./.venv/bin/python scripts/flight/render_flight.py --res 4k --duration 10m --out ~/Desktop/flight_4k.mp4
./.venv/bin/python scripts/flight/render_flight.py --res 256 --duration 30 --out /tmp/flight_tiny.mp4
./.venv/bin/python scripts/flight/render_flight.py --res 1080p --duration 3h --unique 20m --labels none --out flight_3h.mp4

Every frame is rendered deterministically from the seed, so the same command gives the same film. The ink lines, wood grain and paper are measured in world units, so 256 px and 4K share one composition. Each scene opens and closes on blank paper, which lets --unique loop a shorter render seamlessly to fill hours.
